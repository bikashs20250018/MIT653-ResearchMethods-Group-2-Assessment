# 2. Project Overview (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

## 2.1 Background

Artificial intelligence is increasingly being incorporated into e-learning platforms to provide personalized learning experiences. AI-supported platforms can potentially analyse learner information and provide personalized content, adaptive learning pathways, recommendations, adaptive assessments, and AI-generated feedback.

Personalized learning may allow students to receive learning materials and activities according to their individual learning needs and progress. Adaptive learning algorithms can adjust learning content or difficulty based on learner performance and can recommend suitable learning resources.

However, the effectiveness of AI-driven personalization cannot be assumed solely from the presence of AI technology. Student engagement, motivation, satisfaction, perceived learning effectiveness, quality of feedback, platform design, accessibility, privacy, fairness, and dependence on AI may influence students' experiences.

This research therefore proposes a mixed-methods investigation of students' perceptions and experiences with AI-supported personalized e-learning platforms.

The study will focus particularly on:

- AI-driven personalized content delivery
- Adaptive learning algorithms
- Adaptive assessment
- AI-generated personalized feedback
- Student engagement
- Student motivation
- Student satisfaction
- Perceived learning effectiveness
- Privacy and security
- Algorithmic fairness and bias
- Accessibility
- Dependence on AI

The research is proposed and no main participant recruitment or data collection will begin before the required ethics approval is obtained.

---

# 2.2 Research Objectives (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

The research has the following objectives:

1. To explore the impact of personalization using AI in e-learning settings.

2. To investigate how adaptive content delivery relates to student engagement.

3. To explore the correlation between the use of AI in personalized learning and student motivation and satisfaction.

4. To investigate the association between the use of AI-supported personalized learning and academic or perceived learning performance.

5. To identify students' concerns relating to privacy, algorithmic bias, accessibility, and dependence on AI.

6. To create recommendations for effective and responsible implementation of AI-based personalized learning.

---

# 2.3 Primary Research Question (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

**What is the effectiveness of AI-powered e-learning platforms in delivering personalized learning via adaptive learning algorithms and customized content delivery?**

---

# 2.4 Sub-Research Questions

The study will investigate the following sub-research questions:

1. What is the impact of AI personalization on students' engagement, motivation, and satisfaction?

2. How is personalized adaptive learning related to student learning performance?

3. What are the students' attitudes towards personalized content, adaptive assessment, and AI-generated feedback?

4. What privacy, fairness, accessibility and dependence concerns do students have about AI-powered personalized learning?

5. What are the factors that affect the effectiveness of AI-powered personalized e-learning platforms?

6. How does personalized content delivery influence students' willingness to participate in online learning activities?

7. How does adaptive learning influence students' ability to learn at their own pace?

8. How do students perceive the usefulness of AI-generated recommendations for selecting appropriate learning resources?

9. How does the relevance of AI-personalized learning content affect students' satisfaction with e-learning platforms?

10. How does adaptive difficulty affect students' confidence when completing online learning activities?

11. How do students perceive the accuracy and usefulness of AI-generated feedback?

12. How does the timeliness of AI-generated feedback affect students' perceived learning effectiveness?

13. How do students perceive adaptive assessments in comparison with standardized online assessments?

14. What factors influence students' trust in AI-powered personalized learning systems?

15. How does students' digital literacy influence their experiences with AI-powered personalized learning?

16. How does perceived ease of use influence students' willingness to continue using AI-powered e-learning platforms?

17. How concerned are students about the privacy and security of the personal learning data collected by AI-powered platforms?

18. How do students perceive fairness and potential algorithmic bias in AI-generated personalized learning recommendations?

19. How do accessibility, technology availability, and dependence on AI affect students' experiences with personalized e-learning?

---

# 2.5 Research Hypotheses (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

The quantitative component of the study will investigate the following hypotheses:

**H1:** The correlation between AI-driven personalized content delivery and student engagement is positive and significant.

**H2:** The relationship between the use of AI to deliver personalized content and student motivation is positive.

**H3:** The use of AI-driven personalized learning has a positive correlation with students' satisfaction.

**H4:** The more effective a student perceives adaptive learning to be, the more likely they are to report good learning outcomes.

**H5:** The perceived quality of AI-generated personalized feedback is positively related to perceived learning effectiveness.

These hypotheses describe relationships that will be tested using appropriate statistical procedures. The study will not interpret a positive correlation as proof of causation.

# 3. Methodology 

## 3.1 Research Design (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

The study will use a **mixed-methods research design**, combining quantitative and qualitative approaches.

The quantitative component will collect structured survey responses from students with experience using online learning platforms and, where applicable, AI-supported learning tools.

The qualitative component will collect open-ended student responses and may include semi-structured interviews with a smaller group of participants if interviews are conducted as part of the approved study.

The mixed-methods approach will allow the research team to examine measurable relationships between AI-supported personalization and student experiences while also exploring students' views and concerns in greater depth.

The main areas of investigation will include:

- AI personalization
- Adaptive learning
- Student engagement
- Student motivation
- Student satisfaction
- Perceived learning effectiveness
- AI-generated feedback
- Privacy
- Fairness and algorithmic bias
- Accessibility
- Dependence on AI

# 5. Data Analysis (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

## 5.1 Data Preparation and Cleaning (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

Collected data will be screened before analysis.

The research team will check for:

- Missing responses
- Invalid responses
- Incomplete responses
- Inconsistent responses
- Duplicate responses where identifiable within the approved data collection process

Only data appropriate for the approved research purpose will be retained for analysis.

No fabricated or artificially generated participant responses will be included.

---

## 5.2 Descriptive Analysis (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

Descriptive statistics will be used to summarise the quantitative survey responses.

The analysis may include:

- Frequencies
- Percentages
- Means
- Standard deviations

These statistics will describe participant responses across the study's major constructs.

---

## 5.3 Reliability Analysis (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

Cronbach's alpha will be used for appropriate multi-item constructs to assess internal consistency.

The questionnaire items will be developed based on concepts identified in the literature and aligned with the research questions, objectives, and hypotheses.

---

## 5.4 Correlation Analysis (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

Correlation analysis will be used to investigate relationships between relevant variables.

Pearson correlation may be used where appropriate and where the relevant assumptions are satisfied.

The analysis may examine relationships such as:

- AI-driven personalized content and engagement
- AI personalization and motivation
- AI personalization and satisfaction
- Adaptive learning effectiveness and perceived learning outcomes
- AI-generated feedback quality and perceived learning effectiveness

Correlation results will be interpreted as relationships and will not be treated as proof of causation.

---

## 5.5 Regression Analysis (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

Multiple regression may be used to examine relationships between relevant independent and dependent variables where the data and statistical assumptions support its use.

Regression analysis will be used to investigate the research hypotheses and identify relationships between AI-supported personalization and student experience measures.

---

## 5.6 Qualitative Thematic Analysis (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

Open-ended responses and interview data, if interviews are conducted, will be analysed using thematic analysis.

The research team will systematically identify and organise recurring themes.

Potential themes may include:

- Personalised support
- Convenience
- Improved understanding
- Privacy
- Fairness
- Accessibility
- Dependence on AI
- Trust
- Feedback quality
- Ease of use

Systematic coding will be used to increase consistency in qualitative analysis.

---

## 5.7 Integration of Quantitative and Qualitative Findings (BIKASH SHRESTHA)

The quantitative and qualitative findings will be compared and combined during interpretation.

Quantitative findings will provide measurable evidence regarding relationships between the study variables, while qualitative findings will provide additional understanding of students' experiences, perceptions, and concerns.

The combined findings will be used to address the primary research question and supporting research questions.

# 6. Ethical Considerations 

## 6.1 Informed Consent (MD AZZAD ALI)
Participants will receive clear information about the research before participation.

The participant information will explain:

- The purpose of the research
- What participation involves
- The expected time commitment
- How the collected information will be used
- The voluntary nature of participation
- Participant rights
- Privacy and confidentiality arrangements
- Contact information for the research team

Participants will provide informed consent before taking part.

For an online survey, the consent process will be presented before the questionnaire begins. Participants will only proceed after confirming that they understand the information and agree to participate.

If interviews are conducted, participants will provide appropriate consent before the interview.

## 6.2 Voluntary Participation (MD AZZAD ALI)


Participation will be entirely voluntary.

Participants will not be coerced, pressured, or required to participate.

Participants may decline to answer questions they do not wish to answer.

Participation or refusal will not result in academic or other penalties.

Where withdrawal is possible under the approved study procedures, participants will be informed about how they can withdraw and any applicable limitations relating to withdrawal after anonymisation or aggregation of data.

## 6.3 Privacy and Confidentiality (RAMPUKAR SHAH TELI)
he research team will avoid collecting unnecessary personally identifiable information.

The survey will not request information such as a participant's name unless it is specifically required, justified, approved, and separately managed.

Where participant contact information is required for an interview or other approved research activity, it will be separated from research responses wherever practicable.

Research results will be reported in aggregate form.

Individual participants will not be identified in reports, presentations, or publications unless explicit consent and ethical approval allow otherwise

---

## 6.4 Potential Risks (MD AZZAD ALI)

The study is expected to involve minimal risk.

Possible discomfort may arise when participants consider topics such as:

- Privacy of educational data

Participants will not be required to answer questions that they do not wish to answer.

Participants may not receive a direct personal benefit from participation.

However, participation may provide students with an opportunity to reflect on their experiences with AI-supported e-learning and personalized learning.

At a broader level, the research may contribute to understanding:

- How students experience AI personalization
- Factors associated with engagement, motivation, and satisfaction
- Perceived learning effectiveness
- Student concerns about privacy and security
- Fairness and algorithmic bias
- Accessibility issues
- Responsible implementation of AI-based personalized learning

These are potential contributions rather than guaranteed outcomes.

# 7. AI-Specific Ethical Considerations (RAMPUKAR SHAH TELI)
Because the research focuses on AI-supported education, specific ethical issues will be considered.

## 7.1 Privacy and Personal Learning Data (RAMPUKAR SHAH TELI)
AI-powered learning platforms may process learner information such as performance, interactions, learning progress, and other learning-related information.

The research will therefore investigate students' perceptions of privacy and security without requiring unnecessary access to their private platform records.

The study will not collect participants' personal learning-platform data unless specifically approved and necessary for the research.

---

## 7.2 Algorithmic Bias and Fairness (RAMPUKAR SHAH TELI)
The study will consider students' perceptions of potential algorithmic bias and fairness in AI-generated recommendations.

Participants will be able to express concerns about whether personalized recommendations or adaptive systems may produce different experiences for different learners.

The research will report participant perceptions without assuming that a particular AI system is biased unless supported by the collected evidence and appropriate analysis.

---

## 7.3 Accessibility and Unequal Access (RAMPUKAR SHAH TELI)
The research will consider accessibility and technology availability as potential factors affecting students' experiences with personalized e-learning.

The questionnaire may ask participants about access to appropriate devices, internet connectivity, and educational technologies where relevant to the approved research objectives.

---

## 7.4 Dependence on AI (RAMPUKAR SHAH TELI)
The research will explore students' perceptions of dependence on AI-supported learning systems.

Questions will consider whether students perceive AI as a support for learning or whether they have concerns about excessive reliance on automated systems.

---
