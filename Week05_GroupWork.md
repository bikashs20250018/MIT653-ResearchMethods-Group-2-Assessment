# MIT653 Research Methods – Week 05

## Group Literature Review Finalisation

### Research Topic

**Effectiveness of AI-Driven E-Learning Platforms for Personalized Education: Adaptive Learning Algorithms and Tailored Content Delivery**

### Research Question

**How effective are AI-powered e-learning platforms in delivering personalised education through adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery?**

---

## 1. Completion of the Literature Review

During Week 5, the group continued working on and finalised the Literature Review Report.

The literature review examines the effectiveness of AI-driven e-learning platforms in providing personalised educational experiences through adaptive learning algorithms, personalised content, adaptive assessment, real-time feedback and other AI-supported educational technologies.

The final literature review considers both the potential benefits and limitations of AI-supported personalised education.

---

## 2. Main Areas Covered

The completed literature review covers the following major areas:

1. Personalisation, platform adaptability and student needs.
2. Effectiveness of adaptive learning in personalised education.
3. AI-driven personalisation and adaptive learning.
4. AI-enabled adaptive learning platforms.
5. AI-driven personalised learning and educational technology.
6. E-learning effectiveness and AI personalisation.
7. AI personalisation, academic performance and engagement.
8. AI-driven personalisation and learner engagement.
9. Critical analysis of the existing evidence.
10. Ethical issues and future research directions.

---

## 3. Key Findings

The reviewed literature generally indicates that AI and adaptive learning technologies can support personalised educational experiences.

The main potential benefits identified across the literature include:

- Improved student engagement.
- Increased motivation.
- Improved learner satisfaction.
- Improved academic performance in some contexts.
- Personalised learning pathways.
- Adaptive content delivery.
- Adaptive assessment.
- Real-time feedback.
- Identification of individual learning needs.

However, the literature also indicates that positive outcomes are not consistent across all learners, platforms and educational contexts.
