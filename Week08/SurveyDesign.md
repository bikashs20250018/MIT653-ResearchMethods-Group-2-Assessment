# Survey Design 
 
## Group Research Topic 
 
**AI in Education or E-Learning Platforms for Personalized Education: Analyse the effectiveness of e-learning platforms in delivering personalised education experiences, considering adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery.** 
 
--- 
 
## Research Question 
 
**How effective are AI-powered e-learning platforms in delivering personalised education through adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery?** 
 
--- 
 
## 1. Objectives of the Survey 
 
The primary objective of this survey is to collect quantitative and qualitative information about students' experiences and perceptions of AI-powered e-learning platforms and their personalised learning features. 
 
The survey objectives are: 
 
1. To examine how frequently students use AI-powered or personalised e-learning platforms. 
 
2. To investigate students' perceptions of the effectiveness of personalised content delivery. 
 
3. To examine whether adaptive learning features help students understand learning materials more effectively. 
 
4. To investigate the impact of personalised recommendations on student engagement and motivation. 
 
5. To examine students' perceptions of adaptive assessments and feedback. 
 
6. To identify students' overall satisfaction with AI-powered personalised learning platforms. 
 
7. To identify challenges and concerns associated with AI-driven personalised education, including privacy, accuracy and over-reliance on technology. 
 
8. To collect students' suggestions for improving AI-powered personalised e-learning platforms. 
 
The survey objectives are directly aligned with the group's research topic because they investigate the effectiveness of adaptive learning algorithms, tailored content delivery, learner engagement and overall student experience. 
 
--- 
 
# 2. How the Survey Will Be Conducted and Distributed 
 
## Survey Administration Method 
 
The survey will be conducted as an **online questionnaire**. 
 
The preferred platform will be **Google Forms** because it allows the group to: 
 
- Create structured questions. 
- Use multiple-choice and Likert-scale questions. 
- Include an open-ended question. 
- Distribute the survey electronically. 
- Collect responses in a structured format. 
- Export responses for quantitative analysis. 
 
The survey will be designed to be short and clear so that participants can complete it without excessive time commitment. 
 
## Survey Distribution 
 
The survey will be distributed electronically to eligible higher education students. 
 
Potential distribution channels include: 
 
- University student networks. 
- Student email where permitted. 
- University-related communication channels where permitted. 
- Student groups and online communities where appropriate. 
- Direct sharing of the survey link with eligible participants. 
 
Participation will be voluntary. 
 
The group will not pressure students to participate or repeatedly contact students in a way that could be considered coercive. 
 
--- 
 
# 3. Selected Sample for the Survey 
 
## Target Population 
 
The target population is **higher education students who have experience using e-learning platforms**, particularly platforms that provide personalised learning, adaptive content, recommendations or AI-supported educational features. 
 
## Expected Sample Size 
 
The group aims to obtain approximately **100 valid responses**. 
 
This sample size is appropriate for this tutorial-level survey because it provides a useful number of responses for identifying patterns in students' perceptions while remaining practical for the group to collect and analyse. 
 
## Participant Characteristics 
 
Participants should: 
 
- Be currently enrolled in higher education. 
- Have experience using an e-learning platform. 
- Be familiar with online learning activities. 
- Be willing to provide voluntary responses. 
 
The survey will not require participants to provide personally identifying information unless it is necessary and ethically approved. 
 
## Sampling Strategy 
 
The group will use **convenience sampling** to recruit eligible higher education students who are accessible to the research team. 
 
Where possible, the group will attempt to obtain participants with different levels of experience using e-learning platforms to obtain a broader range of responses.

# 5. Ethical Considerations 
 
The group will consider the following ethical issues when conducting and distributing the survey. 
 
## Informed Consent 
 
Before starting the survey, participants will receive information about: 
 
- The purpose of the research. 
- What participation involves. 
- How their responses will be used. 
- Their right to participate voluntarily. 
- Their right to stop participating. 
 
Participants will proceed only after providing informed consent. 
 
## Voluntary Participation 
 
Participation will be completely voluntary. 
 
Participants will not be forced or pressured to complete the survey and will be able to stop participating. 
 
## Anonymity 
 
The survey will avoid collecting unnecessary personally identifying information. 
 
Names, student IDs, addresses and other unnecessary identifying information will not be collected. 
 
## Confidentiality 
 
Survey responses will be treated confidentially and will only be accessed by authorised members of the research group. 
 
## Data Privacy 
 
The collected data will be stored securely. 
 
Access to the dataset will be limited to the research team, and the information will only be used for the purposes explained to participants. 
 
## Avoiding Harm 
 
The survey questions will be designed to minimise potential discomfort or harm. 
 
Questions will focus on participants' experiences and perceptions of e-learning platforms and will not request unnecessarily sensitive personal information. 
 
## Secure Data Management 
 
The research team will ensure that collected survey data is securely managed and that any reporting of results does not unnecessarily identify individual participants. 
 
These ethical considerations are consistent with the Week 8 lecture, which identifies informed consent, confidentiality/anonymity and secure data privacy as important ethical considerations in survey research.

# 6. Planned Data Analysis 
 
The seven closed-ended questions will produce quantitative data. 
 
The group will analyse the responses using descriptive statistics such as: 
 
- Frequencies. 
- Percentages. 
- Mean scores where appropriate. 
- Response distributions. 
 
The results may be presented using tables or charts to identify patterns in: 
 
- E-learning usage. 
- Perceived effectiveness of adaptive learning. 
- Usefulness of personalised recommendations. 
- Student engagement. 
- Adaptive feedback. 
- Overall satisfaction. 
 
The open-ended Question 8 will be analysed qualitatively. 
 
The group will: 
 
1. Read the responses. 
2. Identify recurring ideas. 
3. Assign initial codes. 
4. Group similar responses into categories. 
5. Identify common themes. 
6. Summarise the main suggestions and concerns. 
 
The quantitative and qualitative findings will then be compared to develop a broader understanding of the effectiveness of AI-powered personalised e-learning platforms.
