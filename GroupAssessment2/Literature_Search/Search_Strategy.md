## 3.4 Search Strategy

The main search strategy combines keywords using Boolean operators.

Example:

"Artificial Intelligence" AND "e-learning" AND "personalized learning" AND "adaptive learning"

Other searches include:

"AI" AND "personalized learning" AND "higher education"

"machine learning" AND "personalized adaptive learning"

"intelligent tutoring systems" AND "learning outcomes"

"adaptive learning" AND "student engagement"
