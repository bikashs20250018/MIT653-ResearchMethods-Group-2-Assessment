# Academic Databases and Search Platforms

## Research Topic

*AI in Education or E-Learning Platforms for Personalized Education: Analyse the effectiveness of e-learning platforms in delivering personalised education experiences, considering adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery.*

## Research Question

*How effective are AI-powered e-learning platforms in delivering personalised education through adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery?*

---

# 8. Note on Database Selection

The group prioritised academic and peer-reviewed literature where possible. Publisher platforms and DOI information were used to verify the authenticity and bibliographic details of selected publications.

ResearchGate was used primarily as an access and research-sharing platform, while Google Scholar was used as a broad academic discovery platform.

The final literature review will critically evaluate the quality, relevance, methodology and findings of each selected source rather than relying solely on the database or platform through which the source was accessed.
