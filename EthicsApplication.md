## 1. Project Overview

### Research Objectives

The objectives of this research are to:

1. Identify how frequently students use e-learning platforms.
2. Examine students' experiences with personalised learning content.
3. Investigate how adaptive learning algorithms support students' individual learning needs.
4. Examine whether personalised content improves student engagement and motivation.
5. Explore students' perceptions of the effect of personalised learning on learning outcomes.
6. Measure students' satisfaction with personalised learning features.
7. Identify challenges and areas for improvement in personalised e-learning, including privacy and accuracy concerns.

### Research Question

**How effective are AI-powered e-learning platforms in delivering personalised education through adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery?**

The research focuses on students' experiences and perceptions of personalised learning, adaptive learning, student engagement, learning outcomes, and satisfaction.

## 2. Methodology

### Research Design

This research will use a mixed-methods approach. Quantitative data will be collected through closed-ended survey questions, while qualitative information will be collected through an open-ended question. This approach will help understand students' experiences and perceptions of personalised e-learning platforms.

### Participant Recruitment

Participants will be recruited from higher education students who have experience using e-learning platforms. The survey will be distributed through university networks, email where permitted, university communication channels, student groups, and relevant online communities. Participation will be voluntary.

### Sample Size

The research aims to collect approximately **100 valid responses** from higher education students who have experience with e-learning platforms.

### Data Collection Methods

Data will be collected using an online questionnaire, such as Google Forms. The questionnaire will contain **8 questions**, including 7 closed-ended quantitative questions and 1 open-ended qualitative question. The questions will cover e-learning usage, personalised content, adaptive learning, student engagement, learning outcomes, satisfaction, and suggestions for improvement.

### Data Analysis Plan

The closed-ended responses will be analysed using frequencies, percentages, response distributions, and means where appropriate. The open-ended responses will be reviewed and grouped into common themes. Possible themes include personalised learning, adaptive learning, student engagement, learning outcomes, satisfaction, technical challenges, and privacy or ethical concerns.

## 3. Ethical Considerations

### 3.1 Voluntary and Informed Participation

Participants will receive clear and understandable information about the purpose of the research before completing the survey. Participation will be voluntary, and participants will be informed that they can choose whether to participate. They can also stop participating at any stage without any negative consequences. Consent will be obtained before participants complete the survey.

### 3.2 Minimising Harm and Discomfort

The research is expected to involve minimal risk because it focuses on students' experiences with e-learning platforms. The survey will avoid unnecessary sensitive or personal questions. Participants will not be required to provide information that could cause psychological, social, or other discomfort. Participants can stop the survey if they feel uncomfortable.

### 3.3 Protecting Confidentiality and Privacy

The research will avoid collecting unnecessary personal information. Survey responses will be kept confidential and used only for academic research purposes. Data will be securely stored, and access will be limited to authorised members of the research group. Where possible, responses will be anonymous or de-identified so individual participants cannot be identified.

### 3.4 Ensuring Fair and Respectful Treatment

All participants will be treated fairly and respectfully regardless of their background, culture, gender, education level, or experience. The survey questions will use clear, respectful, and inclusive language. The research team will avoid discrimination and unequal treatment and will consider possible bias when analysing the results.

### 3.5 Honesty with Professional Colleagues

The research findings will be reported honestly and accurately. The research team will not change, hide, or misrepresent the results. Contributions from group members, supervisors, and participants will be appropriately acknowledged. Any sources used in the research will also be properly referenced.

---

## 4. Data Management

### Data Storage

The collected survey data will be stored securely using password-protected digital storage. The data will be retained only for the required academic and research purposes and in accordance with the relevant institutional requirements.

### Data Security

Access to the research data will be restricted to authorised members of the research group. Password protection and secure digital storage will be used to protect the data from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Personal information will not be shared with unauthorised people.

### Data Disposal

After the required retention period, the research data will be securely deleted from digital storage. Any copies containing participant information will also be securely removed to protect participant privacy.
