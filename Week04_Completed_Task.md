### Research Topic

**AI in Education or E-Learning Platforms for Personalized Education: Analyse the effectiveness of e-learning platforms in delivering personalised education experiences, considering adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery.**

### Research Question

**How effective are AI-powered e-learning platforms in delivering personalised education through adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery?**

---

# 1. Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is increasingly being used in education to support personalised and adaptive learning. AI-powered e-learning platforms can analyse learner information, performance and behaviour to recommend learning materials, adjust learning pathways and provide personalised feedback.

Traditional e-learning platforms generally provide the same content to all learners, whereas adaptive learning systems can modify content and learning activities according to individual learner needs. Machine learning, learner modelling and intelligent tutoring systems are examples of technologies that can support this process.

Previous research indicates that intelligent tutoring and adaptive learning systems can improve learning outcomes and student engagement. However, their effectiveness depends on factors such as system design, implementation, learner characteristics, teacher involvement and the quality of available data.

Therefore, this literature review examines existing research on AI-powered e-learning platforms, personalised education, adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery. It aims to determine how effective these technologies are and identify the major limitations and research gaps.

# 2. Purpose and Aims of the Literature Review

## 2.1 Purpose

The purpose of this literature review is to examine existing research on AI-powered e-learning platforms and their ability to deliver personalised education through adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content.

## 2.2 Aims

The literature review aims to:

1. Examine the use of Artificial Intelligence in personalised education.
2. Investigate how adaptive learning algorithms personalise learning experiences.
3. Examine how e-learning platforms deliver tailored educational content.
4. Analyse evidence regarding academic performance and student engagement.
5. Identify common themes across existing research.
6. Identify limitations, research gaps and unresolved issues.
7. Establish how existing research supports the proposed research question.

# 3. Literature Review Process

## 3.1 Literature Sources

The literature review primarily uses:

- Peer-reviewed journal articles
- Systematic literature reviews
- Scoping reviews
- Meta-analyses
- Relevant empirical research studies

Primary research is used to understand empirical evidence, while systematic reviews and meta-analyses are used to identify broader research trends and evidence.

---

## 3.2 Academic Databases

The selected academic databases/search platforms are:

- Scopus
- IEEE Xplore
- ScienceDirect
- ERIC
- Google Scholar

Scopus and IEEE Xplore are prioritised because they provide strong coverage of Artificial Intelligence, machine learning, adaptive technologies and educational technology research.

## 3.5 Inclusion Criteria

Studies were considered relevant when they:

- investigated AI, machine learning or intelligent tutoring in education;
- discussed personalised or adaptive learning;
- investigated e-learning or online learning;
- examined adaptive algorithms or personalised content;
- reported learning outcomes, engagement or performance;
- were peer-reviewed academic publications;
- were available in English;
- provided sufficient methodological information;
- contributed to answering the research question.

Recent research from 2019–2024 was prioritised, while important foundational studies were also included.

---

## 3.6 Exclusion Criteria

The following sources were excluded:

- non-academic websites;
- blogs and opinion articles;
- duplicate publications;
- studies unrelated to education;
- studies unrelated to personalised or adaptive learning;
- studies focusing only on administrative AI applications;
- sources without sufficient research information.

# 4. Selected Literature

## 4.1 Zawacki-Richter et al. (2019)

**Title:** Systematic Review of Research on Artificial Intelligence Applications in Higher Education – Where Are the Educators?

**Methodology:** Systematic literature review.

**Key insight:** The study identified major applications of AI in higher education, including intelligent tutoring, adaptive systems and profiling/prediction.

**Research gap:** Greater attention is required regarding educators' roles and pedagogical implementation.

**Contribution:** Provides a foundation for understanding AI applications in education and establishes adaptive systems as an important area for further research.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-019-0171-0

---

## 4.2 VanLehn (2011)

**Title:** The Relative Effectiveness of Human Tutoring, Intelligent Tutoring Systems, and Other Tutoring Systems

**Methodology:** Review of experimental studies.

**Key insight:** Intelligent tutoring systems can provide substantial learning benefits and can approach the effectiveness of human tutoring under some conditions.

**Research gap:** Effectiveness depends on the design and interaction level of tutoring systems.

**Contribution:** Provides foundational evidence for investigating the effectiveness of intelligent personalised learning systems.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2011.611369

---

## 4.3 Ma et al. (2014)

**Title:** Intelligent Tutoring Systems and Learning Outcomes: A Meta-Analysis

**Methodology:** Meta-analysis.

**Key insight:** Intelligent tutoring systems can improve learning outcomes by providing individualised instruction.

**Research gap:** Further research is required to understand which ITS characteristics produce the strongest learning benefits.

**Contribution:** Provides quantitative evidence supporting the effectiveness of personalised tutoring.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1037/a0037123

---

## 4.4 Kulik and Fletcher (2016)

**Title:** Effectiveness of Intelligent Tutoring Systems: A Meta-Analytic Review

**Methodology:** Meta-analysis of 50 controlled evaluations.

**Key insight:** Intelligent tutoring produced substantial improvements in test performance compared with conventional instruction.

**Research gap:** Effectiveness varies according to assessment methods, control conditions and implementation.

**Contribution:** Provides strong evidence for evaluating the effectiveness of AI-supported personalised learning.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654315581420

---

## 4.5 Maghsudi et al. (2021)

**Title:** Personalized Education in the AI Era: What to Expect Next?

**Methodology:** Research review/conceptual analysis.

**Key insight:** AI and machine learning can analyse learner characteristics and recommend appropriate content and learning pathways.

**Research gap:** Challenges remain regarding motivation, bias, diversity and social aspects of learning.

**Contribution:** Directly connects AI/ML with personalised content and learning pathways.

Source:

https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.10074

---

## 4.6 Crompton and Burke (2023)

**Title:** Artificial Intelligence in Higher Education: The State of the Field

**Methodology:** Systematic literature review.

**Key insight:** AI applications in higher education have expanded considerably and continue to develop.

**Research gap:** More research is required to understand emerging AI applications and their educational impact.

**Contribution:** Provides an updated overview of AI research in higher education.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-023-00392-8

---

## 4.7 Gligorea et al. (2023)

**Title:** Adaptive Learning Using Artificial Intelligence in e-Learning: A Literature Review

**Methodology:** Systematic literature review.

**Key insight:** AI and machine learning can support adaptive e-learning by personalising educational experiences and potentially improving engagement and performance.

**Research gap:** Technical and implementation challenges remain.

**Contribution:** Directly supports the relationship between AI, adaptive learning and e-learning effectiveness.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci13121216

---

## 4.8 Essa, Çelik and Human-Hendricks (2023)

**Title:** Personalized Adaptive Learning Technologies Based on Machine Learning Techniques to Identify Learning Styles: A Systematic Literature Review

**Methodology:** Systematic literature review covering studies from 2015–2022.

**Key insight:** Machine learning can dynamically identify learner characteristics and support personalised adaptive learning.

**Research gap:** Limited comparison of deep-learning approaches for adaptive personalisation.

**Contribution:** Provides technical evidence about machine-learning algorithms used for personalised e-learning.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3276439

---

## 4.9 Bond et al. (2024)

**Title:** A Meta-Systematic Review of Artificial Intelligence in Higher Education: A Call for Increased Ethics, Collaboration, and Rigour

**Methodology:** Meta-systematic review.

**Key insight:** Adaptive systems and personalisation are major themes in AI higher education research.

**Research gap:** Greater attention is required for ethics, methodological quality and contextual factors.

**Contribution:** Helps evaluate not only whether AI is effective, but also the conditions and risks associated with implementation.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-023-00436-z

---

## 4.10 du Plooy et al. (2024)

**Title:** Personalized Adaptive Learning in Higher Education: A Scoping Review of Key Characteristics and Impact on Academic Performance and Engagement

**Methodology:** Scoping review.

**Key insight:** The review included 69 studies. Academic performance improved in 59% of studies and student engagement increased in 36%.

**Research gap:** Technological and implementation challenges remain.

**Contribution:** Provides recent evidence about the effectiveness of personalised adaptive learning using academic performance and engagement as outcomes.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e39630

# 5. Common Themes

The ten selected articles reveal five major themes:

### 5.1 Personalised Learning

AI can use learner information and performance data to customise educational experiences.

### 5.2 Adaptive Learning

Adaptive systems can modify learning pathways and content according to learner performance.

### 5.3 Improved Learning Outcomes

Several studies report improvements in academic performance and learning outcomes.

### 5.4 Student Engagement

Personalised learning can increase learner engagement by providing content appropriate to individual needs.

### 5.5 Challenges and Responsible AI

Privacy, ethics, algorithmic bias, implementation and teacher involvement remain important concerns.

---

# 6. Research Gaps

The literature identifies several research gaps:

1. Limited long-term evaluation of AI-powered personalised learning.
2. Limited comparison between different adaptive learning algorithms.
3. Lack of standardised measures for evaluating effectiveness.
4. Privacy and ethical concerns surrounding learner data.
5. Potential algorithmic bias.
6. Limited understanding of teacher involvement in AI-supported learning.
7. Differences in effectiveness between educational contexts.
8. Need for more empirical studies comparing AI-powered platforms.

---

# 7. Contribution to the Research Question

The literature provides evidence that AI-powered e-learning platforms can support personalised education through adaptive learning and tailored content.

However, effectiveness is not guaranteed. It depends on algorithm design, platform implementation, learner characteristics, educational context and teacher involvement.

Therefore, the literature supports investigating both:

- the benefits of AI-powered personalised learning; and
- the factors that limit or influence its effectiveness.

This leads directly to the research question:

**How effective are AI-powered e-learning platforms in delivering personalised education through adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery?**
